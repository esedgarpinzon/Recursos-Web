Essay Type 01 — Narrative Personal Experience Story

"The Moment I Realized the Importance of English"

Definition

The narrative essay tells a story with a clear point of view and a chronological sequence of events, where the writer intends to share specific meaningful experiences or actions.

Main Characteristics

Personal Point of View Characters & Setting Sensory Details Chronological Sequence

Personal Reflection

"I first faced difficulties writing this first essay, especially because usually we don’t focus on academic writing; we just learn the language and try to develop good fluency to pass interviews, convince recruiters, or communicate with others. However, through the process of writing this essay, I started to gain confidence when writing more academically."

Essay Type 02 — Descriptive Vivid Imagery & Sensory Details

"A Person Whose Presence I Remember Clearly"

Definition

Descriptive essays create vivid impressions of people, places, and events. It describes everything as if the reader were really feeling and experiencing what is being told.

Main Characteristics

Use of Five Senses Show, Don't Tell Precise Language Mental "Camera" for Reader

Essay Type 03 — Persuasive / Argumentative Convincing & Evidence-Based

"Why Online Lessons Can Be as Good as In-Person Lessons"

Definition

Main goal is to persuade the reader to believe, convince them of a specific viewpoint, and prompt them to take action into a particular point of view.

Main Rhetorical Appeals

Logos (Evidence & Facts) Pathos (Emotional Appeal) Ethos (Credibility & Authority)

Essay Type 04 — Cause and Effect Analysis of Origins & Consequences

"You Don’t Need a Diploma to Get a Well-Paying Job"

Definition

The cause and effect essay describes why something happens and examines the consequences of those actions.

Main Characteristics

Focus on Causes Focus on Effects Direct Causal Links

Essay Type 05 — Compare and Contrast Analytical Comparison

"Online Learning vs. Face-to-Face Learning"

Definition

Shows the differences and similarities of each topic or point of view, helping the reader understand the relationship between each option.

Main Characteristics

Compares Two or More Topics Establishes Relationships Clarifies Distinct Contrasts
